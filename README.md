## 2. Microsoft AI Skills Fest 2026

### [Microsoft AI Skills Fest 2026 – Free Certification Voucher](./microsoft-ai-skills-fest-2026/README.md) 🔴

Complete one eligible, unmodified AI Skills Fest skilling playlist on AI Skills Navigator to earn a free Microsoft Certification exam voucher and a Credly digital badge. Includes a screenshot walkthrough of every step.

- **Active period:** June 8–12, 2026 (UTC)
- **Eligibility:** Age of majority (or guardian consent) and an active AI Skills Navigator account; one reward per person
- **Voucher deadline:** Redeem by 12:00 AM UTC, August 11, 2026
- **Links:** [Register on AI Skills Navigator](https://shorturl.at/EbE8G) · [Voucher claim form](https://aka.ms/aiskillsfest/claim)

## 3. Microsoft Data and AI Certifications

### [Data Days: AI @ Work – Exam Voucher Offers](./data-days-exam-voucher-2026/README.md) 🟢

Register for Data Days and request one voucher on the Certification Vouchers page. Step-by-step screenshots included.

- **Active period:** November 2 – December 15, 2026
- **100% off:** DP-800, DP-700, DP-600 (take the exam by November 30 or December 31, 2026)
- **50% off:** PL-300, DP-900, DP-420, DP-300 (take the exam by January 31, 2027)
- **Eligibility:** Free Fabric Community account; one voucher per person; vouchers cannot be extended
- **Link:** [Register for Data Days](https://community.fabric.microsoft.com/c/campaign-form/page?campaignID=Y2FtcGFpZ24tMTc5MTkwNTc2MDQxMQ)

### [Power BI Data Analyst (PL-300) – 50% Off via DataCamp](./datacamp-pl-300-50-off/README.md) 🔴

Complete the Data Analyst in Power BI career track (17 courses, ~48 hours) on DataCamp Premium to receive a 50% off code for Exam PL-300.

- **Link:** [Data Analyst in Power BI track](https://www.datacamp.com/tracks/data-analyst-in-power-bi)

### [60 Days of Data & AI – Free Fabric/SQL Certification Voucher](./fabric-dataday-free-certification-2026/README.md) 🔴

Watch two Data Days sessions and complete three Microsoft Learn modules to request a 100% voucher for DP-600, DP-700, or DP-800.

- **Active period:** June 15 – August 10, 2026

## 4. Azure Certifications

### [Azure Fundamentals (AZ-900) – 50% Off via DataCamp](./datacamp-az-900-50-off/README.md) 🟢

Use the GitHub Student Developer Pack to get 3 months of free DataCamp Premium, complete the Azure Fundamentals skill track (4 courses, ~8 hours), and receive a 50% off code for Exam AZ-900 by email.

- **Eligibility:** Student status for the GitHub Student Developer Pack; DataCamp Premium required
- **Code validity:** 3–6 months after issue (expiry date in the email)
- **Links:** [Azure Fundamentals track](https://www.datacamp.com/tracks/azure-fundamentals) · [GitHub Student Developer Pack](https://education.github.com/pack)

### [Exam SC-500 (Beta) – 80% Off](./exam-sc-500-beta-discount-2026/README.md) 🔴

Discounted beta exam for the Microsoft Certified: Cloud and AI Security Engineer Associate certification. First 300 candidates, on or before June 8, 2026.

### [Microsoft Certification Week 2026](./microsoft-certification-week-2026/README.md) 🔴

Five-day live training covering 16 certifications, including AZ-305, AI-200, and SC-500, through a Microsoft Learning Partner. *Not personally verified — check independently.*

## 7. Microsoft Applied Skills

[Microsoft Applied Skills](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/applied-skills/) are free, scenario-based credentials that validate a specific skill through a hands-on lab assessment.

### [AI Agent Skills Sweepstakes – 100% Off Certification Voucher](./ai-agent-skills-sweepstakes-2026/README.md) 🔴

Earn one eligible Applied Skills credential (agentic AI, Power Automate, or Power Apps), then submit the entry form for a chance to win one of 900 vouchers for Exam AB-100, AB-900, or AB-620.

- **Active period:** June 15 – July 15, 2026
- **Eligibility:** Legal residents 18+ in eligible jurisdictions; no purchase necessary
